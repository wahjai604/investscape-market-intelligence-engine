## Statistical Outputs Describe the Past, Not the Future

Percentiles, correlations, benchmarks, and data-quality scores in this package describe **historical or currently-observed data only**. Pearson correlation results are explicitly exploratory and never imply causation — see `statistical-risk/correlation.ts`. Forecasting, regression, Monte Carlo simulation, and probability-of-threshold calculations are **not implemented** in this Phase 1 release; only their type contracts exist (`phase2-contracts.ts` in both `statistical-risk/` and `market-intelligence/`). Anything resembling a forward-looking projection is out of scope until Phase 2.
